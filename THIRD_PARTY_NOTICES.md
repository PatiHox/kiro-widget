## Scarlab Icons: ghost

The tray icon is the "ghost" icon from the Scarlab Icons set (Scarlab Duotone Line), adapted:
the body is filled instead of outlined and the face is cut out, with a second variant that has
its eyes shut (`rust/assets/ghost.svg`, `rust/assets/ghost-asleep.svg`). The app embeds them and
swaps their one colour for the status colour when drawing the tray icon.
